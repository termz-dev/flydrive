About Flydrive

Finding a driver you can trust shouldn’t depend on who you know.

In Lagos and Ibadan, most people find drivers through word of mouth. It works until it doesn’t — and when it fails, there’s no one to call.

Flydrive screens drivers properly, lets you choose who drives you, and stays responsible for the booking from start to finish. If you need a car as well, we arrange that too.

Our promise

We tell you exactly where your booking stands. We don’t confirm what we haven’t checked.

How we work

Checked, not claimed

Every status you see is backed by a real check — from driver vetting to payment.

Choice on both sides

You choose your driver. Your driver chooses to accept. No silent swaps.

A person, not a queue

Every confirmed booking has a named handler at Flydrive.
