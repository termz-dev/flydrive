How it works

From request to handover, you always know where you stand.

  1. 1

    Request

    Choose driver-only or car + driver, a package, your dates, pickup area and duties. For driver-only, add your car; for car + driver, pick a vehicle class.

  2. 2

    We check

    We confirm the details and, for car + driver, that a suitable vehicle is free. Inside 3 days’ notice, our team checks availability by hand.

  3. 3

    Choose your driver

    See vetted drivers who are free: first name, blurred photo, years of experience and training status.

  4. 4

    Driver accepts

    Your driver sees the whole assignment first. If they decline, you choose again at no cost. We never swap drivers without asking.

  5. 5

    Pay in full

    Every charge and term is shown before you pay.

  6. 6

    Confirmed

    We verify your payment, confirm the booking and name your handler. You then share the exact meeting point and a phone number.

  7. 7

    Handover

    At pickup and return, you and the driver each record the car’s condition, with notes and photos. Any disagreement goes to your handler.

If something changes

Your driver declines

Choose another driver. You haven’t paid anything yet.

Your driver can’t make it after you’ve paid

You choose: approve a replacement or take a refund.

You need to reschedule

Terms are shown before payment and in your booking.

A safety concern

Use the safety option in your booking. It goes straight to a person, separately from ordinary support.
