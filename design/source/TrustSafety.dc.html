The car

At pickup and return, you and the driver each confirm the car’s condition with notes and photos. For car + driver bookings, the vehicle is matched and checked by our team before it reaches you.

During a booking

Your handler is your first contact. Safety concerns go straight to a person and are handled separately from ordinary changes. Drivers who report a concern or refuse unsafe work are never penalised for it.
